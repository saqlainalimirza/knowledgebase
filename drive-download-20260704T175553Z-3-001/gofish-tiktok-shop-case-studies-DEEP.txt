CASE STUDY 1 — EZ BOMBS
Home / Case Studies / EZ Bombs
================================================================================

Title: EZ Bombs Breaks TikTok "Shop Ads" Record With 30x ROAS for First 90 Days
Tagline: High Heat. Higher Returns.
"EZ Bombs tapped into TikTok Shop early—scaling fast with smart creative, bold strategy, and a performance mindset built to win."

About the brand:
EZ Bombs, the #1 food and beverage brand on TikTok Shop, has created a culinary
revolution by merging a strong passion for authentic flavors with easy cooking.
Born from the love of traditional Mexican dishes and the desire to simplify the
cooking process, EZ Bombs reimagined seasoning with their Birria Bombs—each bomb
is a blend of handpicked spices, capturing the essence of Mexico's rich culinary
heritage.

THE CHALLENGE — "The opportunity behind the obstacle"
Prior to working with Go Fish, EZ Bombs was already a huge hit—consistently
selling out thousands of units in a matter of minutes! Due to the extreme
popularity of the product, the EZ Bombs team soon found themselves struggling to
meet such high demand, which began to limit their sales potential and ad
performance. Enter Go Fish.

Go Fish's challenge was to help resolve the logistical issues with strategic
support and to help put EZ Bombs on the best path to rapid growth using a holistic
marketing strategy.

Agency objectives:
* Scale TikTok Shop presence through affiliate live shopping, short-form video, and product placement strategies.
* Combat negative reviews by increasing brand awareness and positive review count.
* Support and launch event drops with limited time offers to fuel purchases and revenue.

Services: TikTok Shop, Social Commerce

CLIENT QUOTE:
"Working with Go Fish made a huge and immediate impact on our growth. We never
imagined EZ Bombs would be the #1 food and beverage brand on TikTok Shop, but here
we are… Go Fish helped us break the TikTok Shop product launch record!"
— Brandon Castaneda, Founder, EZ Bombs

STRATEGY — "The moves that made the difference"
Our team worked with EZ Bombs to enrich their advertising segmentation, driving
new traffic and retargeting existing customers to increase sales, long-term
conversions, and customer lifetime value (CLV).

Successful ads are often led by optimal organic performance, so our strategies,
suggestions, and implementations focused on a creative process to improve EZ Bombs'
organic performance. We identified opportunities based on industry standards on
TikTok Shop, focusing on direct interest targeting and optimizing top-performing
creatives for each segment.

By fine-tuning EZ Bombs' content distribution and simplifying their segmentation,
we empowered them to sustain a healthy average order value, increase their CLV, and
achieve incredible growth!

RESULTS:
- $5M+ in Q2, with over 245K units sold ("Scaled fast and sold out faster.")
- 30x ROAS for the first 90 days after launch ("Turned ad spend into unstoppable growth.")
- 3.5M in affiliate GMV in Q2 2024 ("Content that didn't just perform — it exploded.")

CASE STUDY 2 — PRIME BITES
Home / Case Studies / Prime Bites
================================================================================

Title: How we Helped Prime Bites Generate $100k via Live Streaming in Under 30 days
Tagline: Bold Creative. Smarter Growth.
"Prime Bites tapped into TikTok Shop early, using scroll-stopping content and
data-backed targeting to scale fast—and drive $1M in revenue in just 90 days."

About the brand:
Prime Bites is a premium protein snack brand redefining indulgence with
high-protein, low-sugar alternatives to nostalgic treats. With over seven years in
the industry, it has sold over 10 million protein brownies and 300,000+ boxes
through TikTok alone. Baked in Miami, the brand ships across the U.S. and Canada,
offering high-quality snacks made from Grade A collagen and whey protein.

Founded by CEO Caesar Bacarella, a first-generation Italian American and two-time
world champion racer, Prime Bites embodies resilience and determination.

THE CHALLENGE — "Making live shopping work harder"
As a brand built on both health and nostalgia, Prime Bites sought to expand its
reach and increase customer engagement through innovative digital marketing. The
goal was to leverage TikTok Shop and livestream shopping to drive sales while
fostering a community-driven experience that balances indulgence and nutrition.
Additionally, the brand aimed to boost new customer acquisition while retaining its
loyal customer base.

Unlocking the Power:
* Prime Bites had a strong, loyal community but had not yet fully explored live shopping. Popular figures Bianca & Mya were well-loved but underutilized in livestreams. The challenge: Launching live shopping in an authentic, engaging, and brand-aligned way.
* Solution: Leveraged Bianca & Mya's personalities to create an interactive, community-driven experience. Host Erin helped convert viewership into purchases through engaging live content.

While Prime Bites had built an engaged audience, conversion remained a key
challenge. Live shopping offered an opportunity to bridge the gap between brand
enthusiasm and actual purchases, but early efforts showed that simply going live
wasn't enough—audiences needed stronger incentives to buy in the moment.

Converting Engagement — clear revenue and engagement benchmarks:
* $40K revenue goal for a single live event
* 10% lift in Gross Merchandise Value (GMV)
* New Arrivals Campaign Live Events
* Showcasing fresh products
* Leveraging limited-time offers and audience engagement tactics to drive immediate action.

Services: TikTok Shop, Social Commerce

CLIENT QUOTE:
"If you're looking for a team that truly understands social commerce and can
deliver real, measurable growth, Go Fish is it."
— Bianca Anastasio, Director of Marketing, Prime Bites

STRATEGY — "Optimizing the path to purchase"
When we started live streaming in October 2024, the community was already engaging
and ordering. We ended the livestream with:
* $3,523.00 in GMV
* 376,200 Live Impressions
* 176 New Followers
* Engagement Rate of 7.38%
* Enter Room Rate of 6.12%

Bundling wasn't just smart—it was strategic. Virtual bundles made it easier to buy
more, boosting both conversion rates and average order value.
* Virtual bundles increased conversion rates and AOV by simplifying purchasing decisions and encouraging multi-item orders.
* Customers saw more value in bundling due to slight discounts on complementary products.
* Live-exclusive bundles created urgency and drove immediate purchases.
* Reduced friction in the buying process led to higher conversion rates.
* Strategic pricing and limited-time availability made bundles a compelling option.
* Boosted total purchase size per customer, maximizing live shopping sales potential.

RESULTS:
- 3,245% Increase in views in the first 4 months after launch.
- 82% Increase in CTR in the first 4 months after launch.
- $40,000 Revenue from a single live event.
- ($100K+ generated via livestream shopping in under 30 days; $1M in revenue in 90 days per intro.)

CASE STUDY 3 — WILLOW BOUTIQUE
Home / Case Studies / Willow Boutique
================================================================================

Title: $100k in 1 Livestream: How Willow Boutique Smashed Their TikTok Shop Live Selling Record
Tagline: Live Selling. Record Results.
"Willow Boutique went all in on TikTok Shop livestreams — pairing real-time
audience engagement with high-performing content to drive six-figure revenue and
sold-out product drops."

About the brand:
Willow Boutique, a fashion company that offers cute, quality clothes at an
affordable price, was one of the original beta testers on TikTok Shop and the
second brand to ever have a purchase made on TikTok Shop US. Having already built a
strong organic TikTok presence, the Willow Boutique team and TikTok Shop approached
Go Fish with their sights set on breaking their live selling record (value of
merchandise sold during one livestream event), which was $40k at the time.

THE CHALLENGE — "High stakes. Tight timeline."
Willow Boutique had bold goals — and a tight timeline. The challenge? Double their
live selling record, build a full campaign strategy in just one week, and execute
it across multiple channels while adapting in real time to TikTok's livestream
algorithm. On top of that, we had to scale fulfillment and prove that a
cross-channel strategy could do more than just show up — it could sell out.

Services: TikTok Shop, Paid Social

CLIENT QUOTE:
"Go Fish's impact on our business has been revolutionary, helping us scale across
various platforms. The introduction to TikTok Shop, a game-changer for us, is a
testament to their keen understanding of market trends and opportunities. The team
at Go Fish goes beyond being a service provider; they are true partners."
— Adam Sommers, Owner, Willow Boutique

STRATEGY — "Not just a drop. A full-funnel launch."
Go Fish launched Willow Boutique's fall collection drop on TikTok LIVE, featuring
exclusive deals only available in TikTok Shop. Our cohesive marketing strategy
leveraged the power of cross-channel promotion to amplify awareness, engagement,
and LIVE sales.

In an expansive approach that rivaled many Black Friday/Cyber Monday (BFCM)
strategies, Go Fish and Willow Boutique pre-promoted the livestream event using
video content, RSVP campaigns, and short-form video ads on TikTok and Meta
(including Instagram), driving cross-platform awareness.

Go Fish also scheduled paid ads on Meta to drive traffic to TikTok during the event
and created two email funnels: one to promote the upcoming event and one reminder
for when the livestream began. Go Fish's SMS strategy featured only one text
message, sent out to Willow Boutique's entire SMS list as soon as the livestream
began.

RESULTS:
- 2,345% YoY increase in Meta revenue. ("Cross-channel strategy turned Meta into a powerhouse for full-funnel growth.")
- 100% YoY increase in TikTok revenue. ("Doubling revenue year over year proved the power of live selling and smart promotion.")
- 10% / 10x Return on ad spend (ROAS). ("Every dollar pulled serious weight—driving impact across platforms.")

Additional outcomes:
Go Fish's strategy and implementation proved highly effective, reaching more than
8,000 viewers at one time and increasing Willow Boutique's revenue from key audience
segments across TikTok, Meta, email, and Google. The livestream exceeded our goal of
selling $100k worth of merchandise through TikTok LIVE, breaking and more than
doubling the boutique's previous live selling record of ~$40k worth of merchandise
sold on one livestream!

The campaign reached more than two million users, and the livestream reached 2.8
million viewers. Willow Boutique and Go Fish reported more than four million
products added to carts in the TikTok Shop. The campaign garnered 7.8k new
followers for Willow Boutique's TikTok channel and raised awareness for the brand,
with 71% of the viewers reached being non-followers.

Through its partnership with Go Fish, Willow Boutique was able to increase its reach
and connect with new audiences while more than doubling their TikTok Shop live
selling record. Our team also helped them grow overall revenue while significantly
reducing cost per acquisition (CPA).
